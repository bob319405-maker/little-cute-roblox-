# Plushie Pals: Game Ideas

Ideas for the actual game: the world, what you do every minute, and what keeps you coming back.
⭐ = build first (needed for launch). Everything else can come in later updates.

---

## ⭐ 0. What makes Plushie Pals different: the Plush Mixer
Every Roblox pet game has eggs and rarities. **In Plushie Pals you can create pets that have never existed before.**

Put **two pets** into the Plush Mixer and get a **brand-new baby** that mixes them:
- It takes the body shape from one parent, slightly squished or stretched.
- Its colour is a blend of both parents.
- Eyes, ears, tail and feet each come randomly from one parent or the other.
- Special parts like snouts, honey pots or the moon get shuffled together, up to 3.
- It gets a mashed-up name: Froggo + Bun Bun = **Frogbun**, Star Kitty + Boba Panda = **Starpanda**.
- **Wild mixes** (1 in 25) also get a surprise part from *any* pet, plus random eyes, and earn 3× coins.

With 11 pets that's **121 parent pairs**, and every baby is slightly different, so **no two players have the same pet**.

### Mixer rules
| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| The Mixer is in your Cottage. It takes 3 minutes and costs coins | Gives coins a use and a reason to wait or come back |
| The parents come back. Each pet can mix once a day | Players never lose their favourite pets, and they log in daily |
| The baby gets the rarer parent's rarity and 1.5× their average coins | Mixing is always worth it |
| Babies can still roll mutations (Shiny, Golden, Evil…) | A "Wild Rainbow Starpanda" becomes a legendary trade |
| Mixed pets can be traded | Unique pets create a whole new trading market |

### Why this could go viral
- **"First Discovery":** the first player on the whole game to make a combo gets their name on the **Mix Book** forever ("Starpanda, first made by Ellie123"). Players will race to be first.
- **Server shout-outs:** "🧪 Sam just made the FIRST EVER Wild Moonchi!"
- **YouTube and TikTok videos write themselves:** "I mixed every pet in Plushie Pals", "Rarest mix ever??", "Mixing two Evil pets…".
- Every mix is a surprise reveal, which is exactly the feeling that keeps people hatching eggs.

### Coming next for the Mixer
- **Generation 2:** mix two mixed pets together, for even weirder babies.
- **Stitch Workshop:** design your own plushie by picking a body, eyes, ears, tail, colours and props. The best designs each week get added to a **Community Egg** that everyone can hatch, with the creator's name on it.
- **Mixer upgrades:** faster mixing, a "lucky mix" with a higher Wild chance, and a choice of which parent's body to keep.

## 1. The world map

A small cozy island you can walk across in about 60 seconds. Each area unlocks with coins, so there's always a "next place" to save up for.

| Area | Unlock | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Cozy Meadow** (spawn) | Free | Egg shop, your Cottage, trading booth, big countdown board for the shop restock |
| **Candy Hills** | 5,000 coins | Sweet Egg shop, candy trees that drop coins, a slide down the hill |
| **Cloud Kingdom** | 50,000 coins | Floating islands you bounce between, Starry Egg shop, Cloud Lamb statue |
| **Moonlit Forest** | 250,000 coins | Always nighttime, glowing mushrooms, secret Moon Egg, best place to find Evil pets |
| **Spooky Swamp** (secret) | Find the hidden key | Only opens during the Blood Moon, Cursed Egg shop |

**Extra details that make the world feel alive:**
- Coins and gems sparkle on the ground and respawn. Pets run over to collect them automatically.
- NPC plushies walk around and say tips ("Psst… Rainbow pets love the Moonlit Forest!").
- A giant **Hatch Statue** in the middle shows the server's rarest hatch of the day.

## 3. Core systems

### ⭐ Hatching
- Eggs go into a **hatching nest** in your Cottage. Bigger eggs take longer: Cozy 10s, Sweet 1 min, Starry 5 min.
- **Reveal animation:** the egg wobbles, cracks, and shakes harder for rarer pets. A rainbow beam means Rainbow, a purple lightning crack means Evil.
- **Server announcement** for Legendary, Mythic, Rainbow or Evil: "🌈 Ellie123 hatched a RAINBOW Moon Bun!"

### ⭐ Pets follow you
- Up to 3 pets follow you at first, and you can unlock more slots.
- Pets hop around you, collect coins, and sit down when you stop.
- Each pet has a little idle trick: Bun Bun hops, Mochi naps, Ducky waddles, and Froggo catches flies.

### ⭐ Growing (works while you're offline)
Baby → Teen → Big (and later Huge).
- Each stage is bigger and earns more coins.
- Feed pets snacks to grow them faster.
- Coming back to "Your pets grew while you were away! +2,340 coins 🎉" is the main reason to come back.

### Pet powers
Every pet gets one small power, so which pets you pick actually matters:

| Pet | Power |
|---|---|
| Bun Bun | **Hop:** you jump higher |
| Mochi | **Nap:** grows 10% faster while you're offline |
| Puddle Pup | **Fetch:** grabs coins from farther away |
| Froggo | **Tongue:** sometimes snags rare gems |
| Honey Bear | **Sweet Tooth:** +20% coins in Candy Hills |
| Ducky | **Swim:** lets you cross water shortcuts |
| Strawbunny | **Berry Boost:** snacks heal 2× |
| Cloud Lamb | **Float:** you fall slowly (glide off cliffs!) |
| Boba Panda | **Sip:** +1 walk speed |
| Star Kitty | **Lucky:** +5% chance of a mutation when hatching |
| Moon Bun | **Moonlight:** doubles Evil chance in the Moonlit Forest |

Evil pets keep their power but get a spooky version, such as **Doom Bun's** hop leaving purple smoke.

### ⭐ Trading
- A trading booth in the Meadow: both players put in pets, both press Ready, then a 3-second countdown.
- A **trade history board** shows the best recent trades, which makes everyone want to trade.
- A **Value Guide** NPC shows rough "worth" so younger kids don't get scammed.

### Pet Index
- A book that collects every pet with every mutation. Missing ones show as shadows.
- Finishing a page gives rewards, such as completing all the Common pets for an extra pet slot.
- Everyone wants to complete it, so it keeps players going for a long time.

## 4. Minigames (playable with friends)
Short games that pay coins and gems. They give friends a reason to play together.

| Minigame | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Plush Drop** | Stand on the right colour pad before the floor drops. Last one standing wins |
| **Egg Catch** | Eggs fall from the sky. Catch them in a basket and avoid the rotten ones |
| **Pet Race** | Your pet runs an obstacle course while you tap to boost it |
| **Hide & Squeak** | One player is the seeker. Everyone else hides as a giant plushie |
| **Boba Toss** | Throw boba pearls into cups for points |

---

## 5. Events

| Event | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Shop Restock** | Every 5 min | New eggs in the shop, with a countdown everyone can see |
| **Plush Rain** | Random, about every 20 min | Coins and plush toys rain for 60 seconds |
| **Blood Moon** 😈 | Random night, about every 2 hours | The sky turns red, 5× Evil chance, the Cursed Egg shows up, spooky music |
| **Lucky Hour** | Every 3 hours | 2× mutation chance for 10 minutes |
| **Starfall Weekend** | Every weekend | Starry Eggs in every shop, shooting stars give gems |
| **Admin Party** | Announced on socials | The developer joins, spawns giant Rainbow eggs and gives away pets |

Events at fixed times make everyone log in at once. That spike in players pushes the game up the charts.

---

## 6. Quests and rewards

- ⭐ **Daily Gift:** a 7-day streak. Day 7 gives a guaranteed Sweet Egg. Missing a day resets it.
- **Daily quests (3 a day):** "Hatch 5 eggs", "Trade once", "Collect 1,000 coins", "Play a minigame".
- **Weekly quest:** "Hatch a Shiny" pays a big reward.
- **Playtime rewards:** free gifts at 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes, which helps players stay longer.
- **Badges:** First Hatch, First Mythic, First Evil, Full Index Page, Survived a Blood Moon.

---

## 7. Your Cottage
- Every player has a little house that's their home base.
- Decorate it with furniture you buy, win or find. Pets nap on the beds you place.
- **Visit friends' cottages** and leave a ❤️. A "Most loved cottage" board shows the winners.
- The hatching nest, snack bowl and pet display shelf all live here.

---

## 8. Customizing your pets
- Hats, bows, glasses and capes for pets. These cost Robux or come from quests.
- **Name your pet**, and the name shows on its tag.
- **Fusion (later):** combine 3 of the same pet to make a Huge version.
- **Purify / Corrupt machine:** turn an Evil pet cute again or a cute pet Evil, for coins.

---

## 9. Leaderboards
Boards in the Meadow:
- Most pets hatched
- Rarest pet owned
- Most coins earned today
- Biggest Cottage ❤️ count

Kids love seeing their name on a board, and they record videos of it.

---

## 10. Codes and socials
- Put **codes** on the game page and in Discord, such as `PLUSHIE` for 500 coins or `EVIL` for a free Cursed Egg.
- Release a new code with every update so players check back.
- "Like the game and join the group" gets a free exclusive pet, a **Group Bun** with a crown.
